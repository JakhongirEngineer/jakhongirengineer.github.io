Privacy Policy

YouSpeak · Effective date: November 25, 2026

In short: YouSpeak has no account, no server of ours, no ads, no tracking, no analytics or advertising library and no AI in the app. Your recordings and progress stay on your device. Section 4 lists everything that leaves it: mainly the subscription check with RevenueCat each time the app opens, crash reports if you let your device share them, and what you choose to share.

YouSpeak ("the app") is an IELTS Speaking practice app for Android phones and tablets, iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is made by PrincipiaForge ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your data, what leaves your device and when, and how you delete it. It covers the app wherever you got it (Google Play or the App Store) and these web pages.

1. Who we are

PrincipiaForge, Tashkent, Uzbekistan. For any privacy question or request, email principiaforge@gmail.com. We are responsible (the "controller") for the little personal data we receive, which is described in section 4.

2. Your voice, the microphone and the camera

  • The microphone is used only when you tap to record an answer or to start a speaking exercise. The app asks for your permission first, and you can turn it off at any time in your device's settings. While it records, the app shows that it is recording.
  • Recordings are saved only on your device, inside YouSpeak's private storage, which other apps can't read. The app measures them on your device: how long you speak, your pauses, your pitch and your speaking rate. There is no speech recognition and no transcript of what you say, and no recording is sent to us or to any service.
  • Some exercises listen without recording. Shadowing's listening steps, Say it, the vocabulary test-out and the mock test's microphone check hear you to show you a result, and keep no audio (on Apple devices the system passes Say it and the test-out through a temporary file, deleted at once).
  • The camera is used only by Mirror, in the pronunciation lab, which shows your face live so you can watch your lips. The app asks for permission first. Nothing the camera sees is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Notifications are used only if you turn on reminders. They are scheduled on your device; no server sends them.

3. What stays on your device

Everything you make in YouSpeak is stored only on your device:

  • your recordings, kept until you delete them;
  • their measurements, your progress, your study plan, your stories, your review cards (with any recordings on cards you add yourself), the transcripts you type, your exam details (your test date, target and official bands, and first language) and your settings;
  • a small usage log of what you do in the app (for example, starting or finishing a day's session, an onboarding step, a reminder choice, whether you allowed the microphone, or that the Access screen opened), kept on your device to measure how the app is used. It holds no words, recordings, bands, dates or measurements, is never sent, and keeps only the last 90 days.

No backup. Your recordings and progress are never put into Google's backup, a phone-to-phone transfer, or an iCloud or computer backup. On Apple devices, the system's backup can include the app's small settings file: it holds your reminders' schedule and, once you subscribe, RevenueCat's copy of your Support ID and subscription status, but no recordings and no progress. So if you uninstall YouSpeak, reset your device or move to a new one, your recordings and progress are gone, and nobody, including us, has a copy. Your subscription comes back through Restore purchases.

4. What leaves your device

Only these, and nothing else:

Purchases

When you subscribe, Google Play or the App Store takes the payment. We never see your card or payment details. To check your subscription when the app opens or comes back to the screen, to show the plans and prices, and when you subscribe or restore a purchase, the app contacts RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., in the United States), the service we use to manage subscriptions. It receives:

  • a random app user ID that the app makes for this install (your Support ID), and the store's receipt or purchase token, with the purchase records RevenueCat then gets from the App Store or Google Play;
  • the app's version and build, your platform, your system version, your device model, your language settings and your store's country or region; on Apple devices, also the device identifier Apple gives each app maker (identifierForVendor);
  • your IP address, which every internet request carries: RevenueCat and the network providers it uses to deliver and protect its service handle it, and RevenueCat uses it to work out your country and then doesn't keep it on your subscription record; and the time the app last checked in.

It receives no name, email address, advertising ID, band, recording or measurement. The app user ID is random; Apple's identifier is the same for all of PrincipiaForge's apps on that device. We don't link either to your name or email; but if you send us your Support ID in an email, we can connect the two. We use this data to give you the subscription you paid for, and to see our own subscription reports (how many people subscribe, renew or start a trial). RevenueCat processes it for us under our agreement with it (its data processing terms), which protects your data at least as well as this policy does, and its own privacy policy. RevenueCat uses its own service providers, in the United States and listed in its data processing terms, to host and run its service: cloud hosting, data warehouses, error tracking, network protection and, for some of RevenueCat's own features, AI providers. Without this check, the app can't confirm your subscription.

What you choose to share

These leave your device only when you tap Share or Copy, and only to the app or person you pick. The app you share with handles them under its own terms and privacy policy. YouSpeak keeps no copy: the temporary file a share needs is deleted the next time the app starts.

  • Ask your own AI: the recording of one answer, or of a mock test (one part or all of it, including your answer when the examiner asks your name), with its questions, its figures and a prompt, for the AI tool you choose. Copy text copies only the text.
  • Deep Review's copy: the question and the transcript you typed yourself, copied to your clipboard.
  • The Daily's share card: an image of the card (an idiom's picture, the game's name and your result) and a link that holds only the date and the idiom.
  • Your voice timeline's clip: a short video, made on your device, with the question and about 15 seconds of your first answer and of a later one, their days and their figures.
  • Tell us how it went: a short message with the Speaking band you entered, which you can edit before you send it.
  • An email to support: sent from your own mail app, only if you send it. It holds your email address, your message, and the app version and build and your system version, which the app fills in and you can change. Support email reaches our Gmail account, so Google, our email provider, holds it under Google's own terms and privacy policy, and may process it in the United States and other countries.

Crash reports

If you allow your device to share analytics with app developers (on Apple devices, Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers; on Android, sharing usage and diagnostics data with Google), Apple or Google may show us crash and performance reports for YouSpeak. The app itself has no crash-reporting, analytics or advertising library.

If you test a pre-release version

On TestFlight, we have the name and email address we invited you with (none if you joined through a public link), and Apple sends us your test version's crash reports and usage data automatically, which may include your name and email address, and any feedback you send. If you test on Google Play, we keep the list of testers' email addresses that Google Play needs for the test.

From the stores

The app's audio (its voices and sounds) is downloaded from Google Play or the App Store as part of the app, under the store's own terms. If a card you add is read aloud by your device's own voice, your device's speech engine reads it, under its maker's terms.

5. What we never do

  • We don't ask you to create an account, and we have no server that stores your data.
  • We put no analytics, advertising or tracking library in the app, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. The only reports we see are RevenueCat's subscription reports (section 4) and the crash reports you let Apple or Google share.
  • We don't send your recordings, measurements or anything you write to any AI service.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade your data, or use it for advertising.
  • We make no automated decisions about you, and build no profile of you.

6. These web pages

principiaforge.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. To deliver a page and keep it secure, they process your IP address, the address of the page you open (a shared Daily's link includes its date and idiom) and your browser's details, under their own privacy policies (GitHub's Privacy Statement, Cloudflare's Privacy Policy). Cloudflare may add its own security check to a page, which can set a security cookie. YouSpeak's pages themselves set no cookies, use no analytics and load nothing from other sites.

7. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your device: until you delete it. In the app, go to Settings › Recordings and privacy to delete recordings older than 30 days, 90 days or a year, or every answer's recording; delete one recording from its menu in Me › My recordings; a review card's own recording goes when you delete that card. Or choose Reset everything, which deletes your plan, progress, stories and every recording. Your progress numbers stay when you delete recordings, until Reset everything. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
  • Your purchase record at RevenueCat: kept while it's needed to give you your subscription and our subscription reports. To have it deleted, email us your Support ID (Settings › Access), which is the only way we can find it, and we'll delete it at RevenueCat. Deleting it doesn't cancel your subscription: cancel that in your store. The store keeps its own record of your purchase under its terms.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as we need to help you, then deleted. Ask us and we'll delete them sooner.
  • Testers' details, crash reports and feedback: kept in Apple's and Google's developer tools for as long as the test needs them, under their retention periods; we keep no copy outside them.

There is no account to delete. We answer any request within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to receive a copy of it, to restrict how it is used or object to it, and to withdraw any consent you gave. Because almost everything stays on your device, you can use most of these rights in the app itself. For the rest, email us; for anything at RevenueCat, include your Support ID.

Our legal bases, use by use:

  • Checking and restoring your subscription with RevenueCat: our contract with you (the Terms of Use).
  • Our subscription reports (how many people subscribe, renew or start a trial): our legitimate interest in understanding and running YouSpeak.
  • Answering your support emails: our legitimate interest in helping you.
  • Crash reports, and for testers TestFlight's reports and the testers' list: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing problems and running the app's tests. Crash reports reach us only if you chose to share them with Apple or Google, which you can change in your device's settings.

Your right to object. Where we rely on our legitimate interests, you can object at any time by emailing us (with your Support ID for anything at RevenueCat), and we'll stop unless the law lets us continue.

Complaints. Tell us first, at the address below with "Privacy complaint" as the subject; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work: in the European Economic Area, your country's authority; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commission.

9. Where data is processed

We are based in Uzbekistan. RevenueCat stores its data in the United States; Google, Apple, GitHub and Cloudflare may process data in the United States and other countries. Transfers to RevenueCat from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and their UK and Swiss addenda in RevenueCat's data processing terms.

10. Security

Your recordings and progress live in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security, such as its passcode. The app's connection to RevenueCat is encrypted (HTTPS). We never receive your recordings, so we can't lose them.

11. Children

YouSpeak is for people preparing for the IELTS Speaking test, aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if we learn that we have, for example in a support email, we delete it. The app never asks anyone to type a name, email address or contact details. As in the real test, a mock test's examiner asks for your name: your spoken answer is recorded on your device like any other answer, you can give any name, and it leaves only if you share that mock (section 4).

When this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
