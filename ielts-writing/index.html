Bring your own AI

How grading works

YouWrite has no AI inside. It prepares the prompt; the AI assistant you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another) does the grading.

1

Write your essay

Answer a Task 1 or Task 2 question in the studio, timed like the real exam.

2

Copy the examiner prompt

One tap copies an examiner-style prompt with the band descriptors and your essay. Paste it into your AI.

3

Paste the grade back

YouWrite reads the band scores, compares them with your own assessment and turns the fixes into flashcards.

Your writing stays yours. YouWrite never sends your essays anywhere. You decide what to paste into your AI.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  • iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → YouWrite → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → YouWrite → Cancel.
I paid, but the app doesn't show Pro

Open Settings → Membership in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you subscribed with. If that doesn't help, email us your Support ID.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On the old phone, go to Settings → Data → Export all data and save the file. On the new phone, go to Settings → Data → Import all data. Your subscription comes back with Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Data → Factory reset erases your essays, decks, settings and streak on this phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything too. We hold no copy, because nothing is stored on our side.

Why doesn't the Listen button play right away?

The read-aloud voice is a one-time download of about 560 MB. Tap Download on the Voice pack card in Learn, ideally on Wi-Fi.
