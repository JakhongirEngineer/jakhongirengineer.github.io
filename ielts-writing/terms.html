2. Description of Service

YouWrite is an offline-first practice application for IELTS Writing Task 1 and Task 2. It requires no account, sign-in, or sign-up. The App contains no AI and does not grade your writing itself; instead, it lets you hand your essay off to a third-party AI tool of your choice for grading and feedback.
